## About Me

Yuanyuan Qi is a PhD student in Data Science and AI (DSAI), supervised by <a href="https://research.monash.edu/en/persons/lan-du/">A/Prof. Lan Du</a>, <a href="https://vinuni.edu.vn/people/wray-buntine-phd/">Prof. Wray Buntine</a> and <a href="https://research.monash.edu/en/persons/joanne-enticott/">A/Prof. Joanne Enticott</a>. Her research lies at the intersection of Active Learning (AL) and Natural Language Processing (NLP), with a specific focus on optimizing Large Language Models (LLMs). Driven by the challenge of increasingly scarce computing resources, she aims to leverage Active Learning principles to develop more efficient and accurate frameworks for evolving AI tasks.
